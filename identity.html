01 / Where the identity comes from

My work connects everyday observation with an interest in natural forms and hands-on exploration. The portfolio brings drawings, objects, and photography together through this way of seeing.

Observant

The blue ball and goal net: finding an image in an ordinary moment.

Material curious

The treehouse and root-based structures: exploring branches, connections, and space.

Imaginative

The plant boxes: developing observations into unfamiliar forms.

02 / Proposed visual language

Ann An

Typography: bold sans-serif headlines, hand-lettered accents, and simple supporting text. The contrast brings together playful expression and clear browsing.

Electric blue / #263DE5Acid yellow / #E8FF70Candy pink / #FF9DC5

Composition: a draggable collage of projects, an orbiting title composition, a page-turning personal notebook, and a small surprise interaction. Visitors explore with curiosity while project images remain easy to open.

Working concept: A Curious Little Universe — an explorable collection of observations, material experiments, and imagined possibilities. This is a direction to discuss, not a finalized identity.

03 / Website references

Julie Peeters

Visit portfolio ↗

Observed: a small name label alongside large, tightly placed images. Takeaway for Ann Z. An: let the work establish the first impression; keep supporting labels restrained.

Rinko Kawauchi

Visit portfolio ↗

Observed: a quiet left-side text navigation and one dominant photograph. Takeaway: use scale and breathing room to encourage close looking.

Nathalie du Pasquier

Visit portfolio ↗

Observed: an illustrated board with varied labels and connecting lines. Takeaway: the interface itself can express the artist’s identity. Explore personal composition without copying the graphic style.
